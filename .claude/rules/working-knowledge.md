# Working Knowledge

- LLM pipelines fail open by default — prefer deterministic validators at boundaries over neural judges at the end [uses: 4]
- Articles are optional curated view layer; sentence embeddings are primary retrieval substrate [uses: 2]
- Wiki article provenance via convention: frontmatter `sources: [slug1, slug2]` + inline `[source-slug]` citations [uses: 2]
- Context shaping is the orchestrator's most impactful job — what each worker sees determines success more than prompt instructions [uses: 1]
- Curated article embedding: type "curated" in knowledge.db, citation markers stripped before embedding [uses: 1]
